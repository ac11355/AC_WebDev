## Tres Leches Cake 
![Tres leches cake with strawberry](tresleches.jpg)
Read below and follow!

## Ingredients 
### Cake base
- Butter
- All-purpose flour
- Baking powder
- Salt
- Unsalted butter
- Sugar
- Eggs
- Vanilla

### Milk Mixture
- Condensed milk
- Evaporated milk 
- Heavy whipping cream
- Vanilla
- Rum
### Frosting
- Whipping cream
- Sugar
- Vanilla

## Cake Base Instructions
1. Pre-heat oven to 325 degrees F. Grease and flour a 13 x 9 inch pan.
2. Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a medium-size bowl. 
3. Beat eggs with electric mixer using medium speed for 45-60 seconds.
4.  Slowly add sugar to the eggs.
5. Reduce speed of mixer, and slowly add melted butter. 
6. Add flour in batches and gently fold with a spatula. 
7. Pour batter into prepared pan. Bake for 30-35 minutes. 
### Milk Mixture Instructions
1. Whisk condensed milk, evaporated milk, and cream together with vanilla and rum in a sauce pan with low heat. 
2. Once the cake is cooled, slowly pour the milk mixture over the cake. Cover the cake and put the cake in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours or overnight. 
### Frosting Instructions
1. Use electric mixer to whisk together heavy cream, sugar and vanilla.
